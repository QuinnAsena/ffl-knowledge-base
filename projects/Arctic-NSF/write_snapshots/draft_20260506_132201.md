# Introduction

Wildfire across boreal North America is expected to intensify under future climate change, leading to a potential shift in forest composition. Two species of spruce (white spruce and black spruce), and two species of deciduous trees (Trembling Aspen and Alaskan birch) dominate the Alaskan landscape, and an intensification of the fire regime is expected to favour deciduous species and possibly make way for novel ecosystems with the introduction of lodgepol pine.
